# Content contract

A scenario records:

- learning objective and learner level;
- communicative context and source provenance;
- input text or audio with stable segment IDs;
- production task and acceptable response dimensions;
- feedback rubric and revision prompt;
- accessible transcript and non-audio fallback;
- content license and third-party notices.

Do not include scraped copyrighted material, personal learner data, or claims that a small exercise certifies proficiency.
