# english-scenarios

Reproducible English learning scenarios, dialogues, audio alignment, and practice fixtures for Boxi English.

> **Status:** scaffold. The first content packs are being designed against `boxi-English/english-explain`.

Initial content packs:

- sentence structure and tense contrasts;
- dialogue repair and clarification;
- pronunciation, stress, and rhythm.

Every scenario should include context, input, a learner task, expected observations, feedback criteria, a revision path, and a transfer prompt. Audio and text must have synchronized identifiers and an accessible transcript.

Original teaching content is licensed under [CC BY 4.0](LICENSE-CONTENT). Third-party text, audio, fonts, and images remain under their own licenses and must be recorded in `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`.
